# Strategic Missing Pieces Registry

**Date:** 2026-05-01
**Status:** Audit Gap / Action Required
**Source:** `docs/business/2026-04-26-rob-atomized-todos.md`

This document serves as the formal registry for the 10 structural gaps ("Missing Pieces") identified in the master strategy. These items represent decisions that must be made and documented before full execution can occur.

## MP-1: Pillar Architecture Taxonomy
- **Gap:** No top-level map ties the Chess, Fitness, and Bridge worlds together.
- **Requirement:** A document defining what is shared and what is separate between the businesses.

## MP-2: Cross-Pillar Content Rhythm Spec
- **Gap:** No production cadence exists for managing both channels.
- **Requirement:** A schedule detailing when to produce/publish chess content vs. fitness content vs. bridge content.

## MP-3: Audience Overlap Analysis
- **Gap:** Unknown whether chess viewers care about fitness (and vice versa).
- **Requirement:** Survey or data analysis to determine overlap, which will drive the decision to merge or separate channels.

## MP-4: Avatar Archetype Canonical Doc
- **Gap:** The "Hokage underdog + dual-discipline persona" is implied across docs but never formally specified.
- **Requirement:** A definitive character sheet/avatar document for Rob.

## MP-5: BODI Exit Transition Messaging Plan
- **Gap:** The Reformation spec mandates an exit from BODI distribution, but lacks a communication plan.
- **Requirement:** A messaging strategy for announcing the pivot without burning bridges with the upline/downline.

## MP-6: Legion of Fitness FB Group Migration Plan
- **Gap:** The existing Facebook group is an asset, but its future (rebrand, wind-down, or migration) is undecided.
- **Requirement:** A tactical plan for moving users to Skool/Whop or a rebranded community.

## MP-7: Email Architecture Decision
- **Gap:** Undecided whether to use a single unified newsletter (Beehiiv) or two separate lists.
- **Requirement:** A final architectural decision document.

## MP-8: Wearable Integration Content Plan
- **Gap:** The affiliate strategy exists (FWS-6), but the content/programming layer for integrating Whoop/Oura data into videos is missing.
- **Requirement:** A content series design (e.g., "How Sleep Affects My ELO").

## MP-9: Cohort Challenge Ritual Design
- **Gap:** Implied quarterly challenges for the fitness community need formal specification.
- **Requirement:** Design documents for paid-entry cohort challenges.

## MP-10: Rob's Actual Time Budget
- **Gap:** Unknown weekly hour allocation.
- **Requirement:** A documented breakdown of current vs. target time allocation to ensure roadmaps are feasible.
