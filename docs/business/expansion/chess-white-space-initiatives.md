# Chess Expansion Initiatives (White Space)

**Date:** 2026-05-01
**Status:** Deferred / Incubating
**Source:** `docs/business/2026-04-26-rob-atomized-todos.md`

This document formalizes the unplanted strategic expansion ideas for Hokage Chess. These represent the "white space" in the market that Rob can occupy once the core funnel is deployed.

## 1. Twitch Live Streaming Setup (CWS-1)
- **Concept:** Hokage-themed live training arcs.
- **Priority:** P1
- **Description:** Live streaming the climb. Allows the community to watch Rob train in real-time, solve puzzles, or do live "boss battles."
- **Next Steps:** Define streaming schedule, overlay design, and moderation rules.

## 2. Discord Community Substrate (CWS-2)
- **Concept:** Free tier + paid Hidden Leaf Village structure.
- **Priority:** P1
- **Description:** A persistent home for the audience. The free tier captures general interest, while the paid tier (Genin Squad, Chunin Dojo, Jonin Mentorship) provides gated access and direct accountability.
- **Next Steps:** Finalize channel layout and role permissions. Deploy bots for ELO verification.

## 3. The "Hokage Scroll" Newsletter (CWS-3)
- **Concept:** Weekly Beehiiv newsletter.
- **Priority:** P1
- **Description:** An email newsletter detailing the week's lessons, annotated PGNs, and community highlights.
- **Next Steps:** Decide whether this is separate from the BODI newsletter (see Missing Pieces) and setup Beehiiv.

## 4. Decoupled Shorts Feed (CWS-4)
- **Concept:** Tactic-of-the-day Shorts strategy.
- **Priority:** P2
- **Description:** High-volume, low-production-cost daily shorts focused purely on tactics/puzzles. Separates the long-form narrative content from algorithmic growth content.
- **Next Steps:** Build a template for rapid recording.

## 5. Podcast/Collaboration Tour (CWS-5)
- **Concept:** Pitch an appearance on the "Perpetual Chess Adult Improver Series".
- **Priority:** P2
- **Description:** Leverage Rob's unique narrative to gain exposure on established chess media.
- **Next Steps:** Draft pitch email detailing the 3-year "Road to 1500" journey.

## 6. Platform Recognition (CWS-6)
- **Concept:** Pitch Chess.com Creator of the Month.
- **Priority:** P2
- **Description:** Official recognition from the largest chess platform.
- **Next Steps:** Compile statistics and narrative hook for pitch.

## 7. The Chessable Course (CWS-7)
- **Concept:** Peer-position locked course design.
- **Priority:** P3 (Horizon)
- **Description:** Once the audience is large enough, launch a course explicitly marketed as "from an adult improver, to an adult improver," following the Solveig Friberg playbook.
- **Next Steps:** Deferred until audience supports independent product launch.
