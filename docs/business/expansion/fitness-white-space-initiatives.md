# Fitness Expansion Initiatives (White Space)

**Date:** 2026-05-01
**Status:** Deferred / Incubating
**Source:** `docs/business/2026-04-26-rob-atomized-todos.md`

This document formalizes the unplanted strategic expansion ideas for BODI/Rob's Fitness coaching. These represent future monetization and community-building levers.

## 1. Unified / Dedicated Newsletter (FWS-1)
- **Concept:** Beehiiv newsletter (fitness-side or unified discipline newsletter).
- **Priority:** P1
- **Description:** Weekly written content capturing email leads and providing deep-dive fitness philosophy. 
- **Next Steps:** Determine architecture (unified with chess vs. separate).

## 2. Paid Community Gate (FWS-2)
- **Concept:** Skool/Whop paid community.
- **Priority:** P1
- **Description:** Migrating the existing "Legion of Fitness" community into a structured, monetized gate.
- **Next Steps:** Define pricing tiers and value proposition for the migration.

## 3. Owned Digital Product (FWS-3)
- **Concept:** First non-BODI asset (PDF/cohort).
- **Priority:** P1
- **Description:** Breaking reliance on Beachbody by selling an owned digital product directly to the audience.
- **Next Steps:** Outline the PDF/cohort syllabus.

## 4. Long-Form Authority Surface (FWS-4)
- **Concept:** YouTube long-form OR podcast.
- **Priority:** P2
- **Description:** A platform for long-form discussions on training philosophy, nutrition, and discipline.
- **Next Steps:** Decide on video vs. audio-only and equipment requirements.

## 5. Coaching Software Backend (FWS-5)
- **Concept:** TrueCoach ($26/mo for 5 clients).
- **Priority:** P2
- **Description:** Professionalizing the backend for 1:1 coaching clients.
- **Next Steps:** Setup trial account and migrate first client.

## 6. Wearable Affiliate Stack (FWS-6)
- **Concept:** Whoop / Oura / Element / AG1.
- **Priority:** P2
- **Description:** Monetizing the fitness lifestyle through high-end affiliate partnerships.
- **Next Steps:** Apply for affiliate programs and outline integration into content.

## 7. Owned Identity Domain (FWS-7)
- **Concept:** robbonavoglia.com
- **Priority:** P2
- **Description:** A central hub for the fitness persona, decoupling the brand from BODI.
- **Next Steps:** Register domain and route to a landing page.
