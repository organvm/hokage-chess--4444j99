## Gating Rules

1. **Manual L1 is the red flag.** If BODI L1 exceeds 5 hours/week after reallocation, do not add Twitch, cohorts, or a second weekly long-form format.
2. **One expansion at a time.** The flex bank can hold Boss Battle, Twitch, FWS-3, BR-1, or MP-9 prep. It cannot hold all five.
3. **Daily means batched.** Daily Shorts is acceptable only if produced from already-recorded material.
4. **Discord stays public-first.** RB-6 is capped at the public rituals until paid-tier demand exists.
5. **Cohort waits for proof.** MP-9 requires at least four weeks of ritual consistency and a known lead pool.
6. **FWS-3 starts as a productized artifact.** First owned product is a PDF/challenge, not an expansive course.
